# EcoSort AI — Official Sources & Regulatory Register

This register documents all primary legislation, statutory rules, central pollution control board guidelines, and state-level notifications used to construct the **EcoSort AI Evidence-Based Waste Knowledge Base** for the Bharat AI-SoC Challenge 2026–27 (Problem Statement 2: Smart Recycling and Waste Segregation).

**STRICT COMPLIANCE DIRECTIVE**: No synthetic, fictional, or guessed waste management facts are permitted. Every entry in the knowledge base is traceable to an official legal or technical source recorded below.

## 1. Primary Statutory Source

### [SRC-01] Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026
- **Document Title**: Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 (Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), Notification S.O. 388(E))
- **Issuing Organisation**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
- **File Number**: F. No. 18/3/2022-HSM
- **Publication / Notification Date**: 27th January, 2026 (Effective from 1st April, 2026; superseding Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016)
- **Local File Path**: `docs/sources/solid_waste_management_rules_2026.pdf` (144 pages)
- **Official URL**: https://egazette.gov.in / https://moef.gov.in
- **Date Accessed**: 5th November, 2026
- **Classification**: Primary Legislation (National Gazette Notification)
- **Relevant Sections & Rules Analyzed**:
  - **Rule 1** (p. 79): Short title and commencement.
  - **Rule 3** (pp. 79–84): Statutory Definitions:
    - `3(e)`: Biodegradable waste (p. 79)
    - `3(l)`: Combustible waste (p. 80)
    - `3(p)`: Decentralised processing (p. 80)
    - `3(q)`: Disposal (p. 80)
    - `3(s)`: Dry waste — defined as waste other than wet waste, sanitary waste, special care waste, including recyclable and non-recyclable waste (p. 81)
    - `3(y)`: Horticultural waste (p. 81)
    - `3(z)`: Inerts (p. 81)
    - `3(ze)`: Material Recovery Facility (MRF) (p. 81)
    - `3(zj)`: Recycling (p. 81)
    - `3(zp)`: Sanitary waste — used diapers, sanitary towels/napkins, tampons, condoms, incontinence sheets (p. 82)
    - `3(zt)`: Segregation — four primary streams at source (p. 82)
    - `3(zw)`: Sorting of paper, plastic, metal, glass (p. 82)
    - `3(zx)`: Special care waste — paint drums, pesticide cans/containers, CFL/tube lights, expired medicines, broken mercury thermometers, waste batteries, needles/syringes, contaminated gauze (p. 82)
    - `3(zy)`: Solid waste scope (excluding clinical bio-medical waste, industrial waste, e-waste, battery waste) (p. 82)
    - `3(zzl)`: Wet waste — organic waste including kitchen, food, vegetable, meat, fruit, flower waste and biodegradable waste (p. 83–84)
  - **Rule 5** (pp. 84–85): Duties of waste generator:
    - `5(1)(b)`: Mandates segregation and storage in **four separate streams at source**: wet waste, dry waste, sanitary waste, and special care waste.
    - `5(1)(c)`: Secure wrapping of sanitary waste in pouches/wrapping material and placement in separate sanitary waste bins.
    - `5(1)(d)`: Construction and demolition waste segregation under C&D Rules 2025.
    - `5(1)(e)`: Horticulture and garden waste separate premises storage.
    - `5(1)(f)`: Strict prohibition against throwing, burning, or burying solid waste on streets, drains, or water bodies.
    - `5(1)(g)`: Street vendor containers for food, cups, cans, peel, coconut shells.
    - `5(1)(k)`: Prohibition against mixing clinical bio-medical waste.
  - **Rule 9** (pp. 87–88): Duties of sorting entities at Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs).
  - **Rule 39** (pp. 104–108): Duties of urban local bodies (ULBs):
    - `39(2)(xii)`: Management plan for sanitary waste via dedicated incinerators or Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facilities (CBWTF).
    - `39(2)(xiii)`: Plan for collection and environmentally sound management of special care waste.
    - `39(4)`: Mandatory door-to-door collection of four segregated streams.
    - `39(19)`: Day-to-day collection of vegetable/fruit/meat/fish market waste and decentralized composting / biomethanation.
    - `39(22)`: Separate collection and composting of horticulture and park waste.
    - `39(23)`: Dedicated transport of segregated wet waste to compost or biomethanation plants.
    - `39(32)`: Public education on 4-stream segregation, sanitary wrapping, home composting.
    - `39(48)`: Setting up of Deposition Centres for special care waste (at least 1 per 5 sq. km in cities/towns).
    - `39(49)`: Sanitary landfill restriction strictly to non-usable, non-recyclable, non-combustible inerts.
    - `39(51)`: Statutory public bin color specification — **Green** for wet waste, **Blue** for dry waste, and **Red** in public toilets for sanitary waste.
  - **Schedule I** (p. 114): Timelines for implementation.
  - **Schedule II** (pp. 114–118): Specifications for sanitary landfills.
  - **Schedule III** (pp. 118–122): Standards for composting, treated leachate, and waste-to-energy incineration.
  - **Schedule IV** (pp. 122–124): Fire prevention and management.
- **Knowledge Base Entries Supported**: All 40 entries across Wet waste, Dry waste, Sanitary waste, and Special care waste.

## 2. Specialized National Regulations (MoEFCC & CPCB)

### [SRC-02] E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022
- **Document Title**: E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 (Notification G.S.R. 814(E))
- **Issuing Organisation**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
- **Publication Date**: 2nd November, 2022 (Effective 1st April, 2023)
- **Official URL**: https://cpcb.nic.in/e-waste/ / https://moef.gov.in
- **Date Accessed**: 5th November, 2026
- **Classification**: Primary Legislation (National Statutory Rules)
- **Relevant Sections**:
  - **Rule 4 & Schedule I**: Categorization of Electrical and Electronic Equipment (IT & telecom, consumer electronics, small appliances).
  - **Rule 6**: Responsibilities of consumers and bulk consumers — mandate to segregate e-waste from municipal solid waste, store safely, and channelize exclusively to registered recyclers, refurbishers, or authorized Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) collection channels.
  - **Rule 13**: Strict prohibition on dumping e-waste in landfills, municipal bins, or open burning.
- **Knowledge Base Entries Supported**: `special_ewaste_cables_chargers`, `special_ewaste_smartphones_tablets`, `special_ewaste_small_appliances`.

### [SRC-03] Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022
- **Document Title**: Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 (Notification G.S.R. 664(E), superseding Batteries (Management and Handling) Rules, 2001)
- **Issuing Organisation**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
- **Publication Date**: 22nd August, 2022
- **Official URL**: https://cpcb.nic.in/battery-waste-management/ / https://moef.gov.in
- **Date Accessed**: 5th November, 2026
- **Classification**: Primary Legislation (National Statutory Rules)
- **Relevant Sections**:
  - **Rule 3 & 4**: Scope covering all chemistries (lead-acid, lithium-ion, nickel-cadmium, zinc-carbon, alkaline) across Portable, Automotive, Industrial, and EV batteries.
  - **Rule 4(1)–(4)**: Responsibilities of consumers — mandate to discard waste batteries separately from other waste streams and deposit them at dedicated collection points, buy-back centers, or authorized recyclers.
  - **Rule 4(5)**: Strict prohibition of battery waste disposal in municipal solid waste bins, municipal open dumps, or incineration.
- **Knowledge Base Entries Supported**: `special_batteries_household_dry_cell`, `special_batteries_rechargeable_li_ion`.

### [SRC-04] Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016 (as amended through 2024)
- **Document Title**: Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016 (Notification G.S.R. 320(E), as amended by G.S.R. 571(E) 2021, G.S.R. 133(E) 2022, and 2024 amendments)
- **Issuing Organisation**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
- **Publication Date**: 18th March, 2016 (Amendments: 12th August 2021, 16th February 2022, 2024)
- **Official URL**: https://cpcb.nic.in/plastic-waste-management-rules/
- **Date Accessed**: 5th November, 2026
- **Classification**: Primary Legislation (National Statutory Rules)
- **Relevant Sections**:
  - **Rule 4(1)(a)–(d)**: Carry bag thickness restrictions (minimum 120 microns) and prohibition of non-recyclable multi-layered plastics without viable recovery pathways.
  - **Rule 4(2)**: Ban on 19 identified single-use plastic items effective 1st July 2022 (plastic sticks for earbuds/balloons, candy sticks, plastic plates, cups, cutlery, straws, trays, packaging films, PVC banners < 100 microns).
  - **Schedule II**: Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) categories (Category I: Rigid plastic packaging; Category II: Flexible plastic packaging; Category III: Multi-layered plastic packaging; Category IV: Certified compostable plastic packaging under IS/ISO 17088).
  - **Rule 6**: Segregation by waste generator and handover to local bodies or registered recyclers.
- **Knowledge Base Entries Supported**: `dry_plastic_pet_bottles`, `dry_plastic_hdpe_containers`, `dry_plastic_pp_containers`, `dry_plastic_ldpe_films`, `dry_plastic_single_use_banned`, `dry_plastic_mlp_non_recyclable`.

### [SRC-05] Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016
- **Document Title**: Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016 (Notification G.S.R. 343(E))
- **Issuing Organisation**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
- **Publication Date**: 28th March, 2016
- **Official URL**: https://cpcb.nic.in/bio-medical-waste-rules/
- **Date Accessed**: 5th November, 2026
- **Classification**: Primary Legislation (National Statutory Rules)
- **Relevant Sections**:
  - **Rule 4 & Schedule I**: Segregation of clinical waste into Yellow, Red, White (translucent puncture-proof), and Blue containers.
  - **Rule 2(2) & SWM Rules 2026 Rule 5(1)(k)**: Explicit boundary: healthcare institutions (occupiers) must not mix clinical bio-medical waste with municipal waste.
  - Household-generated sanitary items (menstrual hygiene, diapers) are non-clinical and regulated under SWM Rules 2026 (Rule 3(zp), Rule 5(1)(c), Rule 39(2)(xii)) for dedicated incineration or processing via Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facilities (CBWTF).
- **Knowledge Base Entries Supported**: `sanitary_diapers_baby_adult`, `sanitary_pads_towels`, `sanitary_tampons`, `sanitary_condoms`, `sanitary_incontinence_sheets`, `special_needles_syringes_sharps`, `special_contaminated_gauze`.
